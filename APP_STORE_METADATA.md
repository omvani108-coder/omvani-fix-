## Description (under 4000 chars)

OmVani is your personal AI spiritual companion rooted in Hindu dharma. Get guidance from the Bhagavad Gita, Upanishads, Vedas, and Puranas — all powered by advanced AI that understands ancient wisdom.

SPIRITUAL AI GURU
Ask any life question and receive thoughtful guidance grounded in Hindu scriptures. Whether you need help with relationships, career decisions, inner peace, or daily living, OmVani's AI draws from thousands of years of spiritual wisdom to offer personalized answers.

SCRIPTURE LIBRARY
Browse the Bhagavad Gita (all 18 chapters, 700 shlokas), key Upanishads, and Patanjali's Yoga Sutras. Read original Sanskrit text with English and Hindi translations. Listen to authentic shloka pronunciation with text-to-speech audio.

DEITY IDENTIFIER
Point your camera at any Hindu deity, temple, or sacred object. OmVani's AI instantly identifies it and shares the mythology, significance, and associated mantras.

BHAJAN & MANTRA LIBRARY
Explore a curated collection of devotional bhajans, mantras, and aartis. Search by deity, occasion, or mood. Perfect for daily worship or meditation.

TEMPLE DIRECTORY
Browse a curated directory of India's most sacred temples. Get directions via Google Maps, history, timings, and visiting information for featured mandirs.

PUJA TRACKER
Build a daily spiritual practice. Track your prayers, meditation, and rituals. Maintain streaks and watch your sadhana grow over time.

KUNDLI ANALYSIS
Enter your birth details for a Vedic astrology birth chart analysis. Get insights into your planetary positions, dashas, and life themes based on Jyotish shastra.

DAILY REMINDERS
Set personalized reminders for morning prayers, evening aarti, meditation, or scripture reading. Stay consistent on your spiritual path.

THREE LANGUAGES
Available in English, Hindi, and Tamil. Switch languages anytime from the app.

Key features that require login:
- AI Chat (requires account to track usage limits)
- Puja Tracker (personal ritual tracking)
- Kundli Analysis (personal birth chart)
- Deity Identifier (uses camera)

Features available without login:
- Scripture browsing (Bhagavad Gita, Upanishads, Yoga Sutras)
- Bhajan/Mantra library
- Temple directory

## Google Play Data Safety

### Data collected:
- Email address (account creation)
- Name (account creation)
- Photos (deity identification — processed, not stored)
- Chat messages (stored for conversation history)
- Puja tracking data (stored for streak tracking)
- Kundli birth details (stored for chart analysis)

### Data NOT collected:
- Location (precise or approximate)
- Financial information (handled by payment processor)
- Device identifiers
- Browsing history
- Contacts, calendar, or phone data

### Data sharing:
- Anthropic (Claude AI) — receives chat messages for AI responses
- Google (Gemini AI) — receives photos for deity identification
- Supabase — stores user data (encrypted, RLS-protected)
- No data is sold to third parties
- No data is used for advertising
